# The $10k "Vibecode" Blueprint

**Build Premium Websites with AntiGravity + Gemini 3**

Thanks for reaching out! The secret to a $10,000 website isn't just the code, it’s the **outcome**, the **positioning**, and the **vibe**.

Most people use AI to build "generic" sites. Here is how you use it to build a premium product in minutes.

---

### 🎯 Step 1: Define the "Outcome"

Before you touch a single line of code, you have to tell the AI what the goal is. A site that "looks cool" is worth $0. A site that "converts" is worth $10k.

- **Don’t say:** "Build me a website for a marketing agency."
- **Do say:** "Build a high-conversion landing page for a marketing agency that focuses on booking 15-minute discovery calls. The main goal is to reduce friction for the user."

### 🕵️ Step 2: The "Competitor Rip"

Don't copy the industry standard—beat it.

- **The Move:** Give Gemini 3 the URLs of your top 3 competitors.
- **The Command:** "Analyze these three sites. Tell me where their messaging is weak and where their design feels dated. Now, build me a layout that solves those problems and stands out."

### ✨ Step 3: The "Premium" Polish

This is where the "vibe" comes in. This is the difference between an AI-generated site and a high-end brand.

- **The Hero Section:** Ask for "Large, bold typography with a clear 5-word hook."
- **The "Feel":** Instruct AntiGravity to add "subtle scroll-reveal animations" and "generous white space."
- **The Messaging:** Tell the AI to "write copy that speaks to the user's biggest pain point immediately."

### 🛠️ Step 4: Refine & Elevate

Inside AntiGravity, don't just accept the first build.

- **Guide it:** "Make the buttons rounded with a slight glow on hover."
- **Sharpen it:** "The spacing here feels cramped. Give the sections more room to breathe."
- **Test it:** Open the live preview window and click through like you are a customer. If it feels clunky, tell the AI to "simplify the navigation logic."

---

### 🚀 The 2-Minute Launch

When the "vibe" is perfect:

1. **Connect to GitHub:** AntiGravity handles the heavy lifting of pushing your code.
2. **Deploy on Vercel:** One click and your site is live on a lightning-fast global network.
