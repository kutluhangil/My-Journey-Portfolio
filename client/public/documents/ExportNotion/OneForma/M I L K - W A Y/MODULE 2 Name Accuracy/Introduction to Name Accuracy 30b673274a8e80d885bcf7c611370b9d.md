In the previous chapter as you have understood the aspects of researching to understand the relevance of the query, now let's proceed to understand how to evaluate the accuracy of a Business/POI name.

**"The Name Accuracy rating is used to evaluate the accuracy of a Business/POI name." 
Your final rating will take into consideration both the accuracy of the Business/POI name and the classification assigned to it. Inaccuracies in either or both of these elements will result in rating demotions.**
